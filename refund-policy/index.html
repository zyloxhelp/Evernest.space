Refund and Replacement Policy

Simple, fair and written for busy parents.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

We want every Evernest parcel to reach you perfect. Because our products are eaten by children, we handle returns with extra care. Here is exactly how cancellations, replacements and refunds work.

If this happensWhat we doTime limit
You change your mind before dispatchCancel and refund in fullAny time before dispatch

1. Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel any time before your order is dispatched. Message us with your order details and we will cancel it and refund any amount you have paid.
  • Once an order has shipped, we may not be able to stop it. If you refuse delivery or the parcel comes back to us, we will refund the amount you paid for the products after the parcel reaches our warehouse.
  • Orders that have already been delivered cannot be cancelled. Please see replacements below.

2. Returns

For the safety and hygiene of children, we do not accept returns of opened or used products, and we do not accept change of mind returns after delivery. Unopened, sealed products can only be returned if we have approved it in writing first.

3. Replacements

We will happily replace your order if you received:

  • the wrong product,
  • a missing item from your order,
  • a damaged, leaking or tampered pack, or
  • a product that is expired or very close to expiry.

How to ask for a replacement

  1. Contact us within 7 days of delivery at hello@evernest.space.
  2. Share your name, order number and phone number.
  3. Send clear photos or a short unboxing video that show the outer box, the product, and the batch and expiry label.
  4. Our team may ask a few more questions before approving. We usually reply within 2 business days.
  5. Once approved, we ship a replacement at no cost to you. It is created as a new order with a new order number.

4. Refunds

  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card, wallet or bank account).
  • Refunds usually reflect within 5 to 7 working days after we approve them. Your bank or wallet may take a little longer to show it.
  • If a payment was deducted but your order was not created, the amount is normally reversed automatically within 5 to 7 working days. If it is not, write to us with your payment reference and we will sort it out.
  • Shipping fees already paid are not refunded, unless the issue was our mistake.

5. What is not covered

  • Opened or partly used products, unless they arrived damaged, wrong or expired.
  • Products damaged after delivery through storage, heat, water or misuse.
  • Requests raised after 7 days of delivery.
  • Taste preference. Kids can be picky, and we are sorry if it is not a hit! Tell us what your child thought and we will do our best to help.

6. If your child has a reaction

Please stop using the product and speak to a doctor first. Then tell us what happened, with the batch number from the pack, so we can look into it carefully.

Need help? Write to hello@evernest.space. Please include your order number so we can help faster.
