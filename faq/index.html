About Evernest

What is Evernest?

Evernest is a kids nutrition brand with fun, tasty supplements: Kids Protein Powder, Kids Eye Health Gummies with DHA, and Multivitamin Gummies.

Who can use Evernest products?

Gummies are for children aged 3 and above and the protein powder is for children aged 4 and above. Please supervise gummy time and check with your paediatrician if your child has any medical condition, allergy, or takes any medicine.

Are the products safe?

We design our products for children and keep our claims simple and honest. Always follow the suggested use on the pack, and read the allergen information before you start.

Do you use my information for anything else?

We only use your details to process and support your order and to share updates. Read our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Products

How many gummies or scoops should my child have?

One gummy a day for the gummies, and one serving a day for Kids Protein Powder. Please never exceed the suggested use.

Can my child take more than one Evernest product?

Many families do, but we suggest checking with your paediatrician before giving more than one supplement.

Will it change my child's eyesight or make them taller?

No supplement can promise that. Evernest products support normal nutrition as part of a balanced diet. They do not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition.

Where can I find the ingredients and allergen information?

On the product page and printed on every pack. Please read it before use.

Orders and delivery

How do I place an order?

Choose your product and pack size, then tap Buy now or Add to cart. Review your cart, tap Checkout and share your contact details. Our team will confirm your order and delivery address with you.

How long will delivery take?

We dispatch in 1 to 2 business days. Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days depending on your pin code.

Is delivery free?

Delivery is free on orders above Rs. 499. A small fee of Rs. 49 applies on smaller orders.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your parcel ships we send tracking details by SMS, email or WhatsApp.

Do you deliver all over India?

We deliver to most pin codes in India. If we cannot reach yours, we will let you know right away.

Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, PhonePe, Paytm, Google Pay, debit and credit cards, RuPay and net banking.

Is it safe to pay online?

Payments are handled by trusted payment partners. We never see or store your card details.

Returns and refunds

Can I return a product?

We do not accept returns of opened products or change of mind returns, because the products are consumed by children. We do replace items that arrive wrong, missing, damaged or expired.

How do I ask for a replacement?

Message us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and clear photos. Our Refund Policy lists the exact steps.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, any time before it is dispatched. Just message us with your order details.
