good questions

Protein FAQs

Which age is it for?

Kids Protein Powder is made for children aged 4 and above. If your child has a medical condition, allergy or special diet, please check with your paediatrician first.

Can it replace a meal?

No. It is a supplement to a balanced diet and never a replacement for real food.

How do I make it?

Measure 15 g of powder, add it to a glass of milk and stir well. Please do not mix it into hot drinks.

What flavours and sizes are there?

Chocolate, Strawberry and Cookies and Cream, each in a 200 g, 400 g or 800 g tub. Pick your flavour and size above.

How many servings a day?

One 15 g serving a day is the suggested use. Always follow the serving size printed on the pack.

Does it have added sugar?

Evernest Kids Protein Powder has no added refined sugar, no added artificial sweeteners and no added preservatives. Please read the label on your pack for full ingredient and allergen details.

What vitamins and minerals does it have?

18 vitamins and minerals per serving, including Calcium, Iron and Vitamin D. The full nutritional information is on the product page and on the pack.

Do you have any allergen information?

Please read the allergen information printed on the pack before use, and check with your paediatrician if your child has any allergies.
