Nationally, CNNS 2016-18 found vitamin D deficiency in 18.2% of 5 to 9 year olds and vitamin B12 deficiency in 17.3%. Numbers vary by city, age and survey, but the pattern is the same: a daily multivitamin helps fill gaps a busy plate can leave.

Sources

  1. Journal of Nutritional Science (2023): dietary intake and micronutrient deficiency in 2,428 urban school children aged 6 to 16 in ten Indian cities, data from 2019-20
  2. Nutrients (2023): vitamin B12 and folate deficiencies in Indian children and adolescents, Comprehensive National Nutrition Survey 2016-18
  3. UNICEF Knowledge: vitamin D deficiency among Indian children and adolescents, Comprehensive National Nutrition Survey 2016-18

good questions

Multivitamins FAQs

Is it safe for a 3 year old?

It is made for children aged 3 and above. Always supervise young children while they eat gummies, and check with your paediatrician if you are unsure.

How many gummies a day?

One gummy a day, chewed well. Please do not exceed the suggested use on the pack.

Can we take it with Kids Eye Health Gummies?

They are designed as separate daily supplements. If you plan to give more than one supplement, please check with your paediatrician first.

Can it replace fruits and vegetables?

No. Multivitamins support a balanced diet but never replace it.

What vitamins and minerals does it have?

11 essential vitamins and minerals: Vitamin C, E, B5, B12, A and D, Folic acid, Zinc, Magnesium, Iodine and Sodium. See the nutrition table on this page.

Is it vegetarian?

Yes, the gummies are 100% vegetarian. Please check the label on your pack for full ingredient and allergen details.

Where can I see the nutrition facts?

On this page, and on the pack along with the full ingredient list and allergen information.
