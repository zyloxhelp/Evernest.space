Eyesight FAQs

Who is it for?

Children aged 3 and above. Please check with your paediatrician if your child has a medical condition or allergy, or takes any medicine.

How many gummies a day?

Give 1 to 2 gummies a day. Please do not exceed the suggested use.

Will it fix my child's eyesight?

No. Kids Eye Health Gummies give nutrients that support normal vision as part of a balanced diet. They do not treat or correct any eye condition. Regular eye check ups, outdoor play and screen breaks matter too.

What is Algal DHA?

Algal DHA is a plant based source of the omega-3 DHA. It supports brain development and eye health.

What do Lutein and Zeaxanthin do?

They are carotenoids found naturally in the eye. They support clear vision and help filter blue light.

Is it vegetarian?

Yes, the gummies are made to be 100% vegetarian. Please check the label on your pack for full ingredient and allergen details.

Where can I see the full ingredient list?

On the back of the pack, along with the nutrition facts and allergen information.
